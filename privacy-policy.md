# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 13, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Konstantin Talipov ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Flufi mobile application ("the App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 1. What Information We Collect

**Information stored locally on your device.** The App is designed to work without requiring an account. Habit data you enter — such as quit dates, check-ins, mood entries, notes, and streak history — is stored locally on your device only. We do not transmit, collect, or have access to this content on our own servers.

**Analytics information.** We use Firebase Analytics (provided by Google) to understand general, aggregated usage patterns — for example, which screens are used and how often, to help us improve the App. This does not include the personal habit content described above (your notes, quit dates, or mood entries are never sent as analytics data).

**Crash and diagnostic information.** We use Firebase Crashlytics (provided by Google) to receive automated crash reports if the App encounters an error, so we can identify and fix bugs. These reports may include technical device information (device model, OS version, and the state of the App at the time of the crash) but do not include your personal habit content.

**Subscription and purchase information.** If you purchase a Premium subscription, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store, and subscription status is managed via RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your payment card details — these are handled entirely by Apple. RevenueCat receives limited purchase-related information (such as your anonymized App Store transaction identifiers) necessary to validate and manage your subscription.

## 2. Will Your Information Be Shared With Anyone?

We do not sell your information. We share limited information only with the service providers described above (Google Firebase, RevenueCat, and Apple) to the extent necessary for them to provide analytics, crash reporting, and subscription management on our behalf. These providers are contractually and technically restricted from using this information for their own independent purposes beyond providing these services.

We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

## 3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The App is a mobile application, not a website, and does not use browser cookies. The App does not use advertising identifiers (IDFA) and does not serve third-party ads.

## 4. Social Logins

The App does not offer or require sign-in through any social media account, Apple ID, Google account, or any other third-party login service. There is no account creation of any kind.

## 5. International Data Transfers

The limited analytics and crash data described above may be processed by Google and RevenueCat on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States. These providers maintain their own safeguards for handling such data in accordance with applicable law.

## 6. How Long We Keep Your Information

Habit data stored locally on your device remains there until you delete the App or clear its data — we have no way to retrieve or delete this data on your behalf, since we never receive it. If you uninstall the App without a backup, this data may be permanently lost (see the App's Terms of Service for details).

Analytics and crash data are retained by Google Firebase according to Google's standard retention periods, after which they are automatically deleted or aggregated.

## 7. How We Keep Your Information Safe

Because your personal habit content stays on your device and is never transmitted to us, its security depends primarily on your own device's security (passcode, Face ID, or other device-level protections). We rely on the security practices of Google Firebase and RevenueCat for the limited data those services process on our behalf.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights

Since we do not collect or store your personal habit content, there is generally no personal data held by us for you to request, correct, or delete beyond what is described in this policy. If you have questions about the limited analytics or crash data associated with your device, or wish to make a request regarding it, you can contact us at the email below.

## 9. Do-Not-Track Signals

Because the App does not track you across third-party apps or websites for advertising purposes, we do not currently respond differently to Do-Not-Track browser signals.

## 10. California Residents

If you are a California resident, you may have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what limited categories of information (if any) are processed on our behalf by service providers, and to request that such information be deleted. You can exercise these rights by contacting us at the email below.

## 11. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older, consistent with our Terms of Service. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided information to us, please contact us so we can address it.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
