Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely through Apple, not through Flufi directly. On your iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select Flufi, and tap Cancel Subscription. You'll keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Flufi, go to the paywall screen, and tap Restore at the bottom. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your habit data — quit dates, check-ins, mood entries, and notes — is stored locally on your device and never leaves it. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

How do I delete my data?

Since everything is stored locally, uninstalling Flufi permanently deletes all of your data. There's currently no separate in-app reset — please back up anything you'd like to keep before uninstalling.

Can I track more than one habit?

The free version lets you track one habit at a time. Flufi Premium unlocks tracking multiple habits at once, plus full stats and personal insights.

I'm struggling right now — where can I get help?

Flufi is a self-tracking tool and isn't a substitute for professional support. If you need to talk to someone, the in-app "Need support right now?" card on the Settings screen links to findahelpline.com, which can connect you with a crisis helpline in your country.
